Welcome to my corner of GitHub. I spend most of my daily life deep in **C/C++**, **Bash**, and **Linux**, while keeping things running on Windows. I prefer working with modern standards (like C++23), but I have a huge soft spot for the past. 

### ⚡ What I'm up to:
- 🛠️ **Reinventing the wheel:** I love building custom libraries from scratch and conceptualizing my own programming languages.
- 👾 **Retro Tech:** Frequently messing around with MS-DOS, FreeDOS, and old-school Windows environments.
- 🔍 **Security & Reversing:** Doing some light decompilations, and very occasionally hunting for vulnerabilities to satisfy the chaotic part of me.
- 🌍 **Localization:** Bringing English projects over to the Polish language.
